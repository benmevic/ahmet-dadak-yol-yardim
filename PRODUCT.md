# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, static output, no admin panel (user confirmed). District and service pages generated from data.

## Users

Drivers in İzmir whose vehicle has broken down, been in an accident, has a flat tyre, a dead battery or an empty tank, or who have been drinking and need their car taken home. They are usually on a phone, often at the roadside, sometimes at night, under stress. Their job: reach a tow operator they can trust, fast.

## Product Purpose

The website for Ahmet Dadak Yol Yardım, a 7/24 tow and roadside assistance business. Success means a visitor calls or sends their location on WhatsApp within seconds, and that the business ranks in local searches like "Bornova çekici" or "<ilçe> oto kurtarma".

## Positioning

An owner-operator (Ahmet Dadak) with his own flatbed tow truck, based in Bornova and Kemalpaşa, serving all of İzmir. The proof is real: photos of actual jobs on İzmir streets, not stock imagery.

## Operating Context

Contact is by phone call or WhatsApp (including sharing a live location). Visitors arrive mostly from Google search and Google Maps on mobile. The owner will not edit the site himself.

- The user chose the light, trust-building direction ("B") for the site and asked that it must not look generic or AI-made.

## Evidence on Hand

Real job photos of the tow truck (Fiat Ducato flatbed, plate 35 CDV 546) in `kaynak/gorseller/`: Park Bornova at sunset, a Maserati on the highway, Karşıyaka–Bornova dolmuş in rain and on a city street, a Peugeot Partner under an overpass, sedans on residential streets, the truck with ramp down. Also a yellow/black promotional flyer. No testimonials, review counts or job statistics exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Calling or sending a location is always one tap away, on every page.
- Show, don't claim: real jobs and real places carry the trust.
- Speak to someone stressed at the roadside: short, plain Turkish.
- Local first: every district and service is findable by the words people actually search.
